# ADR 0007: Licensing & Open-Source Compliance

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Drift must be freely usable, redistributable, and legally safe for organizational use.

## Decision
- Drift is licensed under MIT license (same as D2).
- All dependencies are open-source, vendored, and air-gap ready.

## Consequences
- No proprietary dependencies.
- Supports internal and external usage.
